ResNet-50 vs ConvNeXt-Tiny, layer by layer

The two backbones in our d96 runs: 1e (resnet50.tv_in1k) and P1-cnx (convnext_tiny.fb_in22k_ft_in1k_384). Everything else is identical: data, labels, augmentation, attention head, optimiser, schedule, SWA. Sizes below are for one 384×384 training window (3 adjacent slices, 140 mm crop, ≈ 0.36 mm per pixel). Knee images are synthetic drawings for intuition, not patient data.

1 · The whole journey

Both shrink the image 32× in total and end with one vector per window. The differences are in how each stage is built and where the depth goes.

3 · The repeating block: the core difference

ResNet's 3×3 conv decides where and what in one step through a 64-channel pipe. ConvNeXt separates them: first gather a wider view (7×7 depthwise), then reason richly at each spot (1×1 convs in a 384-channel space).

4 · What "depthwise" means

Depthwise convs are cheap, which is what lets ConvNeXt afford 7×7 kernels: 49 weights per channel instead of 9 × 64 per output channel.

This may matter most for findings judged over a region (effusion, OA joint-space narrowing, synovitis) rather than at a single point.
